# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Competitive gamers use EasyRes while preparing for or moving between matches. They need fast, dependable control over stretched and native display modes without navigating a full GPU control panel.

## Product Purpose

EasyRes makes supported Windows display modes quick to inspect, save, and switch. Success means users can understand the active display state, apply the intended resolution with minimal friction, and safely recover to native settings without surprising monitor-state changes.

## Brand Personality

Precise, tactical, fast. The product should feel focused and trustworthy under pressure, with direct language and immediate feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid RGB-heavy gamer software, neon cyberpunk decoration, and bloated GPU control panels. Avoid interfaces that obscure important display state behind ornamental dashboards or ambiguous labels.

## Design Principles

- Make current display state and the next action unmistakable.
- Optimize common switching workflows for speed without hiding safety consequences.
- Keep resolution changes and monitor power-state changes explicitly separate.
- Use concise, technically accurate language rather than marketing claims.
- Preserve expert control while keeping recovery actions easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA where applicable to a desktop Qt application. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, non-color state cues, and reduced-motion behavior.
